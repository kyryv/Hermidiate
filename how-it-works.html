How it works

From the first message to a slot in the technician's calendar

Five steps, the same every time. The AI reads the customer's words; fixed rules pick the technician and the time.

  1. 01

    The customer writes

    They describe the fault in their own words. AI picks out the equipment, job type, urgency and preferred time.

  2. 02

    Details are checked

    It asks for name, phone, e-mail and address in turn, and checks the address on a map, district included.

  3. 03

    A chosen technician

    Based on skills for that equipment, the area the technician covers and their daily job limit.

  4. 04

    Free time is found

    It searches the calendar, allows for travel and offers three slots. An exact requested time comes first.

  5. 05

    The visit is booked

    It re-checks the slot, creates the event in the technician's calendar and sends the customer an invitation.

When it is not enough

Unclear and unsafe cases go to a person

Installations and late changes

These go to your dispatcher with the reason, the contact and the last message attached.

Gas leak or smoke

The assistant does not schedule. It tells the customer to call 112 and alerts the dispatcher immediately.

Outside your area or trade

The assistant politely declines, so no visit is booked that your technicians cannot take.
