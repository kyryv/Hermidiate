Service booking chat

When a customer books a service through the Hermidiate chat, we process what is needed for the booking: name, phone, e-mail, the address of the equipment, the fault description, the chosen time and the conversation itself.

  • Purpose: taking the request, choosing a technician, booking the appointment and handling changes.
  • Retention: conversations and data are deleted automatically after 12 months at the latest.
  • Controller: when the chat runs on a service company's website, that company is the controller and Hermidiate processes the data on its behalf as a processor.

Who we share data with

We use these providers (processors):

  • website hosting [e.g. Netlify],
  • Google (technicians' calendars; the fonts on this website load from Google Fonts, which passes on your IP address),
  • n8n (runs the chat automation),
  • OpenAI (processes the message text with a language model),
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (address check).

Some of them may process data outside the EU, mainly in the USA. In that case the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.
