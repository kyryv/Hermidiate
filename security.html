Security & privacy

AI reads the text. Fixed rules handle the schedule.

For a product that handles customer contacts and calendars, these are the questions to answer first. The full legal wording is in the privacy policy.

What we process

Only what a booking needs

Customer dataname, phone, e-mail, address of the equipment, fault description, chosen time and the conversation itself.
Whytaking the request, choosing a technician, booking the visit and handling changes.
How longconversations and data are deleted automatically after 12 months at the latest.
Who is the controllerwhen the chat runs on a service company's website, that company is the controller and Hermidiate processes the data on its behalf.
Cookiesthis website uses no cookies and no analytics tools.

Who else touches the data

Service providers

Calendarthe technicians' calendars where visits are booked.
OpenAIprocesses the text of the message with a language model.
n8nruns the chat automation.
OpenStreetMap / Nominatimchecks the address.
Website hostingserves this website.

Some providers may process data outside the EU, mainly in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Safety

Unsafe requests are never scheduled

A gas leak or smoke mentioned in the chat stops the booking flow. The customer is told to call 112 and the dispatcher is alerted at once. Unclear cases go to a person with the full context.

Your rights and how to exercise them are described in the privacy policy. You can also contact the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.
