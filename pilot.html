Questions

Before you book

Does it work with our calendar?

Google Calendar is supported today, and each technician gets the job in their own calendar. Using another calendar or dispatch system? Write to us and we'll find a way.

Which languages does it speak?

Czech, English and Ukrainian. Customers can mix languages in one conversation.

What happens when the AI is not sure?

Unclear cases, installations and late changes go to a person, with the reason, the contact and the last message attached. Gas leaks and smoke are never scheduled.

How long is the pilot?

We agree the length on the first call, based on your services and the number of technicians.
