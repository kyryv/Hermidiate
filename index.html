How it works

From the first message to a slot in the technician's calendar

  1. 01

    The customer writes

    They describe the fault in their own words. AI picks out the equipment, job type, urgency and preferred time.

  2. 02

    Details are checked

    It asks for name, phone, e-mail and address in turn, and checks the address on a map, district included.

  3. 03

    A technician is chosen

    Based on skills for that equipment, the area the technician covers and their daily job limit.

  4. 04

    Free time is found

    It searches the calendar, allows for travel and offers three slots. An exact requested time comes first.

  5. 05

    The visit is booked

    It re-checks the slot, creates the event in the technician's calendar and sends the customer an invitation.

Features

It handles the cases that make the phone ring today

Address and district check

It verifies the address on a map. If the customer gives the wrong Prague district, it asks. If they write only "Prague", it fills the district in.

No double bookings

Same phone or e-mail and same equipment with a future visit? The customer sees the existing booking instead of making a new one.

Move or cancel without a call

Customers move or cancel their visit themselves up to 24 hours before it, even from a new chat window, found by phone number.

Hand-off to your dispatcher

Installations, late changes and unclear cases go to a person. The alert lands in a calendar with the reason, contact and last message.

Safety first

If a customer mentions a gas leak or smoke, the assistant does not schedule. It advises calling 112 and alerts the dispatcher immediately.

Only your area, only your services

The service area follows where your technicians actually go. Outside it, or outside your trade, the assistant politely declines.

Fits your company

All you need is your technicians' calendar

Each technician gets the job straight into their own calendar, with the address, contact and fault description. We set up working hours, holidays and qualifications together with you at launch. Technicians install nothing new.

Before the visit the technician calls the customer, clarifies the scope of work and shortens the reserved time if needed.
