Use cases

Built for companies that send technicians to the customer's door

We started with heating and cooling, but the same flow works in many trades. What changes is the set of questions, the skills each technician needs and how long a visit takes.

It fits when all four are true

  • A customer gets in touch with a problem or a request.
  • You send a person to the customer's address.
  • The right technician depends on skills and area.
  • The visit goes into a calendar and may be moved or cancelled.

Heating and cooling Ready now

Where Hermidiate started

Air conditioning, heat pumps, boilers and ventilation, for companies with 10 and more technicians. Summer brings AC peaks, winter brings boiler calls, and technicians in the field can't pick up the phone.

Heat pumps

Fault codes such as E21, no-heat emergencies and yearly service. The assistant asks the questions the technician needs before the visit.

Boilers

No-heat calls and routine maintenance. Anything that smells of gas is not scheduled and goes straight to a person.

Air conditioning

Seasonal peaks bring many requests at once. Each one is matched to a technician with the right skills and a free slot.

Ventilation

Inspections and filter changes are planned around each technician's day, with travel time included between jobs.

Related trades Short setup

Same flow, your job types

During setup we add your job types, the skills or certificates each technician has and how long each visit takes. The booking rules stay the same.

Plumbing, heating and gas

Emergencies and planned repairs alike. The right technician is chosen by type of work, water, gas or heating, and by area.

Electricians and inspections

Regular electrical and gas inspections with fixed deadlines, planned by area and by each technician's licence.

Chimney sweeps

Hundreds of seasonal chimney checks a year, grouped by town and street. Also for one or two-person businesses.

Solar, batteries and wallboxes

Service visits and installations, with technicians chosen by certification and region. Installations can span several days.

Appliance repair

Washing machines, dishwashers and fridges, including warranty repairs. The technician is matched by brand and area.

Security, cameras and garage doors

Installations and service calls, with the technician chosen by the system they know.

Drains and locksmiths

Emergency call-outs that need a fast response: the nearest free technician gets the job.

Pest control, cleaning and pools

Repeat visits planned by area, so each technician's day follows a sensible route.

Companies with a partner network Let's talk

One request, the right partner

For companies that don't send their own technicians but pass the job to a network of partner firms. These need a connection to your systems, so we plan them with you.

Manufacturers and distributors

A customer reports a fault and gets the nearest authorised service partner with a free slot, including warranty repairs.

Property managers and facility management

A tenant reports a fault, the right tradesperson gets the job, and the manager sees every request in one place.

Shops that sell with installation

Customers buy an air conditioner or a kitchen and choose an installation time with a partner installer right away.

Insurers and assistance services

A damage report, such as a water leak or a broken heating system, goes to a partner tradesperson by trade and region.

Energy suppliers

Boiler checks, solar and wallbox installations through partner installers.

Developers and construction firms

Defects reported in new flats go to the right technician or subcontractor by type of defect.

Internet and TV providers

Installations and repairs at the customer's home, assigned by each technician's area.
